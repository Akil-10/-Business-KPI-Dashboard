Customer Churn KPI Dashboard

15 synthetic customers. Use the filters to slice every KPI and chart. Orange = churned or at risk, blue = retained.

KPI definitions

  • Churn rate = churned customers ÷ customers in the selection.
  • Retention rate = 1 − churn rate.
  • Revenue at risk = monthly charges of churned customers (lost monthly recurring revenue), shown as a share of the selection’s total monthly revenue. The dataset has no “active” status, so this is revenue already lost, and annualised ×12 for scale.
  • Tenure KPIs = average tenure in months, overall and split by churned vs retained.
  • Tenure bands: 0–6, 7–12, 13–24, 25+ months. With n=15, treat every figure as indicative.
